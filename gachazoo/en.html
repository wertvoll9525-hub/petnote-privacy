Gacha Zoo Privacy Policy

Gacha Zoo ("the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the app handles.

1. Information the app collects

The app has no sign-up or login and does not collect personal information such as your name or contact details. Your collection, draw history, animal names and settings are stored only on your device.

If Android Auto Backup is enabled, this data may be included in your Google account backup. This is a Google feature, and the developer cannot see its contents.

Whether you have already received the welcome tickets (a single timestamp) is stored in Google Block Store, so it stays after you uninstall and reinstall the app. It is kept only on your device and in your Google backup, and is never sent to the developer.

When you share a card, the image is created temporarily on your device and sent only to the app you choose.

2. Time check

To keep the daily draw limit accurate, the app connects to Google servers (google.com) only to read the current time. No information is sent to the developer.

3. Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob rewarded ads. AdMob may collect the advertising ID, device information, approximate location (based on IP) and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. See How Google uses information from sites or apps and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads.

Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads.

4. Notifications

Only if you turn on reminders in Settings, the app shows a notification on your device at the time you choose. Nothing is sent externally for this.

5. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

6. Deleting data

Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored on your device.
