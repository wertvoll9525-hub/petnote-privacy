Pet Notebook Privacy Policy

Effective: October 19, 2026 · Developer: Hankan

Pet Notebook does not collect your information.

1. No information collected

Pet Notebook does not send or collect any personal information to the developer. The app has no internet permission.

2. Information stored only on your device

Pet information you enter (name, photos, birthday, weight, health and vet records, expenses, etc.) is stored only on your device. The developer cannot see it.

3. Photos

Only photos you pick are copied into the app's private storage. The app does not access your whole gallery.

4. Notifications

The app asks for notification permission for reminders and medication alerts. You can still use the rest of the app if you decline.

5. Backup files

A backup file is created only when you make one, and you choose where it is saved. You are responsible for managing backup files.

6. No third parties, ads, or analytics

The app uses no ads, analytics tools, or external services.

7. Deleting data

Deleting a pet in the app, or uninstalling the app, removes all data from your device.

8. Children

This app is not directed at children and does not collect children's information.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will announce it on this page.
